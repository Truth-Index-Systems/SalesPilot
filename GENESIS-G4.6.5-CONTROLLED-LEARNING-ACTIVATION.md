# Genesis G4.6.5 — Controlled Learning Activation & Freeze

This release closes G4.6 by turning mature channel outcomes into guarded guidance.

- Market evidence never overrides route availability or silently replaces the verified primary route.
- Five completed engagements creates an emerging signal; fifteen creates a mature signal.
- Review shows the observed channel, sample size, response rate, meeting rate and guidance.
- Duplicate outcomes are rejected and WON/LOST are terminal.
- Scheduler refreshes learning guidance every cycle.
