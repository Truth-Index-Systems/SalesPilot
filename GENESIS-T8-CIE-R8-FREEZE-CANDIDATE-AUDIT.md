# CIE-R8 Freeze Candidate Audit

## Audit 1 — Relationship Theory Re-audit: PASS
The end-to-end relationships remain coherent:
Evidence → AI semantics → CE1 canonical representation → Truth qualification → epistemic/temporal state → Commercial Reality → deterministic commercial decision → graph route authority → contact binding → research/recourse → MarketRoute workflow.

No release was found where a numeric compatibility score is mathematically required to express the governing relationship. Categorical accessibility, truth/epistemic qualification, constraint state, Pareto route reasoning, route-derived contact binding, and decision-value research remain the correct relationship representations established in CE2/CIE R1-R7.

## Audit 2 — Cross-system Adversarial Audit: PASS
Verified fail-closed properties include:
- shadow output cannot control behaviour;
- blocked/unresolved route cannot become execution route;
- weighted contact confidence cannot outrank the contact participating in the authorised route;
- missing/contradictory decision-critical knowledge cannot silently become commercial force;
- engagement confidence cannot override CIE commercial/route/contact authority;
- dormant AI route selector throws if accidentally reconnected;
- legacy opportunity scorer throws if accidentally called;
- research directives cannot widen the existing governed repair contract;
- counterfactual recourse cannot create evidence or mutate reality.

CIE-R8 static: 13/13 PASS.
CIE-R8 adversarial: 5/5 PASS.
Full inherited CE2-R8 → CIE-R7 chain: PASS.

## Audit 3 — Architecture / Authority Audit: PASS
Single-authority rule is satisfied for the migrated decision categories.
Legacy opportunity scoring is SHADOW only.
Legacy route/contact numeric values are compatibility/presentation telemetry only.
AI commercial route selection has no live caller and is hard-failed.
CIE-R4/R5/R6/R7 own commercial decision, route, contact and research authority respectively.
MarketRoute owns workflow/presentation and may not manufacture commercial ranking.

## Audit 4 — Code / SQL Reachability Audit: PASS WITH HISTORICAL ARTIFACTS
Historical migrations still contain old score equations and columns. They are retained as database history and compatibility schema, not live mathematical authority.
Current R8 SQL removes engagement-confidence threshold authority from autopilot and checks CIE-R4, CIE-R6 and CIE-R5 route provenance instead.
Current contact canonicalisation no longer uses routingScore to decide which channels survive a persistence ceiling.
Live application pages no longer present opportunity_score as the reason an opportunity is recommended.
Changed TypeScript/TSX files: no parser errors in isolated compiler syntax audit.
