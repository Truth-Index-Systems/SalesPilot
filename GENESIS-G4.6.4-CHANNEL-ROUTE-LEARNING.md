# Genesis G4.6.4 — Channel & Route Learning

This release records real commercial outcomes against the exact engagement, route quality and primary channel used.

## Outcomes
NO_RESPONSE, REPLIED, MEETING_BOOKED, QUALIFIED, WON and LOST.

## Learning safeguards
- Outcomes can only be recorded after execution is complete or an email is sent.
- Tenant membership and write role are enforced in the RPC.
- Channel comparisons require five completed engagements before being labelled a learning signal.
- This release exposes evidence but does not automatically rewrite live scoring weights.

## Founder Dashboard
Adds response, meeting and win totals plus channel-level response rates, meeting rates, route quality and sample readiness.
