# Genesis T8 — CE2-R8 Counterfactual Decision Calculus

## Purpose
R8 answers: what explicit actionable changes are sufficient to move a Commercial Reality to a declared target condition, and which successful intervention plans are irreducible and nondominated?

## Research selection
Three candidate families were tested:
1. nearest counterfactual / distance minimisation;
2. scalar-cost actionable recourse;
3. subset-minimal intervention sets plus Pareto burden ordering.

Nearest-distance fails when the closest mutation is inadmissible or unactionable. Scalar cost requires unjustified exchange rates between unlike burdens such as action count, irreversibility, money, and time. R8 therefore adopts the third candidate while retaining the actionable-recourse principle that only feasible actions count.

## Constitutional model
- Upstream semantics define conditions and intervention effects.
- Truth Index remains owner of truth.
- CE2 consumes only explicit `satisfiesConditionIds`; it never fabricates causal effects.
- Only ACTIONABLE interventions can produce recourse.
- Successful intervention sets are reduced to subset-minimal plans.
- Pareto comparison is non-compensatory across action count, irreversibility, unknown reversibility, known money cost, and known duration.
- Unknown cost/duration is never treated as zero.
- Search limits fail closed rather than silently truncating plans.

## Deferred
R8 does not learn causal effects, infer intervention feasibility, attach expected utilities, or predict success probabilities. Dynamic causal recourse can be considered only when Genesis has a governed causal/action model.
