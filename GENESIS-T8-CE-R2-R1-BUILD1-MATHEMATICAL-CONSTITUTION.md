# Genesis T8 CE-R2 — Release 1 / Build 1
## Mathematical Constitution

### Status
Implemented as a pre-equation constitutional layer. This build does not define UDOSIB numeric equations, weights, scoring functions, propagation arithmetic, coherence arithmetic, or opportunity ranking arithmetic.

### Purpose
CE-R1 froze how Genesis T8 represents truth-qualified commercial knowledge. CE-R2 defines deterministic commercial reasoning over that frozen knowledge. R1 Build 1 freezes the laws that future mathematics must obey before any equation is introduced.

### First Principle
Commercial reasoning does not maximise opportunity. It eliminates commercial realities that cannot exist until viable realities remain, then later CE-R2 releases may deterministically order the survivors by mathematically defined coherence and stability.

### Responsibility Boundary
- AI owns semantic interpretation, ontology mapping, constraint identification, semantic dependency mapping and human-language explanation.
- TI / authorised Truth Authority owns truth qualification, confidence, coverage, evidence dependency, contradiction and freshness.
- UDOSIB owns deterministic constraint reasoning, survival/elimination, commercial state derivation and eventual survivor ordering.
- Applications own presentation and product-access rules.

### Opportunity Constitution
An Opportunity is a truth-qualified commercial reality between a seller, an offering and a target organisation that survives all applicable Boundary Constraints and exhibits sufficient commercial coherence to justify engagement.

An organisation is never intrinsically a good opportunity. Opportunity is relational and depends on the seller, offering, target, truth-qualified graph state and evaluated time.

### Constraint Constitution
The only constitutional classes are:
1. BOUNDARY — violation makes the evaluated commercial reality non-viable.
2. LIMITING — restricts a surviving reality without independently eliminating it.
3. SUPPORTING — independently supports coherence but cannot override a failed boundary.
4. UNKNOWN — reduces certainty, not possibility.
5. CONTRADICTORY — consumes Truth-Engine-qualified contradiction state; commercial significance is local to the active reasoning dependency.

R1 Build 1 defines semantics only. Equations are deferred to CE-R2 R2.

### Mathematical State Constitution
The named state variables are Commercial Coherence, Capability Compatibility, Operational Compatibility, Technical Compatibility, Constraint Pressure, Knowledge Sufficiency, Reasoning Confidence, Commercial Stability and Opportunity Classification.

These are derived reasoning, never authoritative knowledge, and must always be recalculable from canonical inputs.

### Mathematical Purity
Future CE-R2 kernels may consume canonical CE-R1 graph identity, truth authority identity, ontology identity, temporal scope and structurally validated AI constraint contracts. They may not consume raw prompts, raw LLM responses, provider metadata, UI state, database layout, application workflow state or unqualified knowledge.

### Directed Research Constitution
Unknown constraints do not become negative facts. Later CE-R2 research intelligence will identify the decision-relevant unknown that would most improve the commercial decision, send that research objective to AI, route newly discovered knowledge through TI, and then recalculate UDOSIB from the updated qualified state.

### Explainability
Every conclusion must be reconstructable through deterministic trace nodes back to applied constraints, canonical relationships, canonical tokens and truth qualification. AI may explain that trace in human language but may not rewrite mathematical causality.

### Determinism
Identical canonical seller, offering, target, graph, truth authority, ontology, temporal scope and validated constraint inputs must produce identical mathematical state when evaluated by the same frozen algorithm version.

### Frozen CE-R1 Boundary
This build is intentionally additive. It does not modify the CE-R1 CKR v1.0 kernel or TI-2.1.8. The CE-R2 mathematics namespace has its own entrypoint under `lib/genesis-t8/mathematics/`.
