# Genesis T8 Philosophy

Genesis T8 is a deterministic reasoning platform for AI-derived knowledge.

## The separation

1. **AI owns semantics.** AI reads, interprets, resolves semantic equivalence, maps evidence into the canonical ontology, resolves entity meaning, and proposes relationships. Deterministic code does not infer meaning.
2. **Truth authority owns truth.** The currently authorised authority is the frozen TI-2.1.8 engine. It alone qualifies evidence-backed knowledge for probability, confidence, coverage, contradiction, dependency and freshness.
3. **Genesis reasoning engines own mathematics.** They consume only canonical, truth-qualified knowledge and authorised upstream reasoning. They do not consume raw prompts, raw AI output, provider metadata, UI state or database layout.
4. **Applications own presentation and workflow.** Applications consume Genesis; Genesis does not depend on applications.

## Knowledge and reasoning

Knowledge persists. Reasoning is derived and recalculated. A cached reasoning result is operational convenience, never authoritative knowledge.

Tokens represent atomic propositions. Relationships are first-class graph assertions. Nine dimensions are independent lenses over the same knowledge, not mutually exclusive buckets. Historical truth is preserved; current truth is a temporal view, not an overwrite.

## Why this exists

AI is excellent at semantic interpretation and weak as an invariant mathematical authority. Deterministic code is excellent at invariants and mathematics and weak at open-ended semantics. Genesis T8 deliberately gives each side the responsibility it is best suited to perform.

The platform therefore aims to make AI-derived knowledge usable by reproducible, explainable reasoning engines without turning model intuition into a hidden score.
