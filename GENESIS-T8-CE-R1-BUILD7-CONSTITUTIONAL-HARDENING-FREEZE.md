# Genesis T8 CE-R1 Build 7 — Constitutional Hardening & CKR v1.0 Freeze

## Purpose

Build 7 closes every freeze-blocking issue identified by the ten-audit CE-R1 constitutional review. It introduces no Commercial Fit mathematics and does not modify TI-2.1.8 mathematics or production MarketRoute runtime behaviour.

- Genesis T8 Constitution and responsibility boundaries;
- Commercial Token Theory;
- the 9D Commercial Graph;
- Commercial Genome Ontology;
- AI Research Contract;

## Build 7 closure groups

### 1. Constitutional hardening

Semantic Sovereignty is explicit: AI owns semantics. Mathematical Purity is explicit: reasoning engines consume canonical, truth-qualified platform representations only. The engine registry is extensible; adding a future engine does not amend the Constitution.

### 2. Token integrity

Runtime enum/type validation is explicit. Canonical values use type-aware serialization. Evidence IDs are nonblank and unique. Token object IDs are distinct from semantic proposition identity. Canonical proposition duplicates are rejected. Temporal intervals are validated. Superseded tokens identify their successor. Predicate-definition fingerprints preserve ontology meaning.

Predicate cardinality distinguishes SINGLE_CURRENT, MULTI_CURRENT, TEMPORAL_SERIES and EVENT_SET facts.

### 3. Graph and relationship integrity

Edges and projections have canonical semantic identities. Duplicate semantic graph objects are rejected even when object IDs differ. Undirected edge identity is direction-normalised. Edge class and direction are runtime validated. Relationship types are registered with class, direction and topology rules. Acyclic relations are cycle checked while legitimate commercial cycles remain legal.

The graph boundary recursively validates tokens. CURRENT_AUTHORITATIVE, AS_OF and HISTORICAL temporal views are explicit.

### 4. AI → Genesis hard boundary

A raw AI candidate cannot become a token. The complete research envelope must first pass deterministic cross-reference validation and receive the runtime validated-envelope brand. Runtime canonical value validation occurs before candidate conversion. AI still owns semantic mapping; deterministic validation checks only contract conformance.

### 5. Truth authority independence

TI-2.1.8 remains the sole active truth authority and remains frozen byte-for-byte. CKR no longer requires the truth implementation to be structurally hard-coded forever; a versioned authority registry provides the platform boundary. No authorised truth authority means authoritative reasoning fails closed.

### 6. Audit-driven ontology refinements

The v1 ontology now includes first-class commercial capability predicates, organisation-unit separation, corporate hierarchy roles, legal versus delivery geography, mandatory regulatory obligations versus voluntary standards, and procurement process concepts. Dynamic interpretation remains outside the ontology.

### 7. Scale and time

The logical graph no longer prescribes physical database representation. A persistence-neutral access port defines indexed retrieval semantics. Global Genesis entity identity is application/provider independent. Evidence linkage and refresh work are explicit operational contracts. Reasoning cache identity includes engine, algorithm, graph state, truth authority, ontology semantics, time scope and upstream snapshots.

## Freeze rules

CKR v1.0 is append-only after freeze. Existing semantic meanings may not be silently repurposed. Breaking semantic changes require an explicit new CKR version. TI-2.1.8 remains independently frozen.

CE-R2 may add deterministic UDOSIB-style commercial mathematics, but it may not mutate CKR v1.0 to manufacture desired scores.
