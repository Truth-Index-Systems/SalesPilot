# Genesis post-freeze — Breadth-first Market Scan → Depth Research + phone presentation

## Scope

This patch changes only Route Intelligence dispatch priority and presentation of persisted switchboard/phone reachability. G4 evidence rules, pass limits, opportunity assembly, G5 intelligence, approval and execution remain unchanged.

## Route Intelligence orchestration

The scheduler still permits one heavyweight Route Intelligence AI call per invocation. Claim priority is now:

1. Continue an already-focused depth account when due.
2. Otherwise give every eligible approved company its first Route Intelligence pass (`route_expansion_pass = 0`).
3. Once the first-pass market scan is complete, promote the strongest incomplete account into depth focus and finish it before selecting another account.
4. If new companies arrive while a depth account is active, they do not interrupt it. After the focus account becomes READY/EXHAUSTED, pass-0 companies regain priority.

A new `contact_discovery_sessions.depth_focus_started_at` marker persists the depth account across scheduler invocations and quota deferrals.

## Phone / switchboard presentation

`commercial_routes.channelValue` remains authoritative. Wherever the Opportunity/G5 workspace presents a SWITCHBOARD route, the actual persisted phone number is displayed with the route instead of a bare channel label.

No phone number is inferred or generated by G5.
