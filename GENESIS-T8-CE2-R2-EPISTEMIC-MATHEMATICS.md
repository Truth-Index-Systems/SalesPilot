# Genesis T8 — CE2-R2 Epistemic Mathematics

## Status
CE2 Evolution Build 2. Additive post-freeze layer. Frozen CE-R2/UDOSIB 1.0.0 and TI-2.1.8 are consumed read-only.

## Purpose
Separate epistemically different knowledge conditions that CE2 v1 necessarily compressed into generic knowledge deficit/unresolved state.

## Epistemic vector
Each knowledge item is represented across five orthogonal categorical axes:
- presence: PRESENT / MISSING
- verification: VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED
- resolution: KNOWN / UNCERTAIN / UNKNOWN
- contradiction: CONSISTENT / CONTRADICTORY
- temporal validity: CURRENT / EXPIRED / UNASSESSED

MISSING uses NOT_APPLICABLE on all other axes because no proposition exists to verify, resolve, contradict or temporally qualify.

## Derived primary states
KNOWN, UNCERTAIN, UNKNOWN, UNVERIFIED, CONTRADICTORY, EXPIRED, MISSING.

The primary state exists only for deterministic control flow. It never replaces or discards the full vector.

## Commercial permission
- KNOWN: may supply frozen UDOSIB directional force.
- UNCERTAIN: may supply frozen UDOSIB directional force with uncertainty.
- CONTRADICTORY: contradiction channel only.
- UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED / EXPIRED / MISSING: zero directional commercial force.

No probability, confidence, contradiction severity, freshness or decay is calculated here. Truth Index remains authoritative for truth mathematics.

## Research disposition
Each unresolved state maps categorically to its appropriate research action: acquire, discover, verify, resolve uncertainty, resolve contradiction, or refresh. R2 does not rank research.

## Temporal boundary
EXPIRED is accepted only as an upstream categorical qualification. Deterministic time, age, decay and validity-window mathematics are deferred to CE2-R3.
