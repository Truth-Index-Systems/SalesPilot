# Genesis G3.5 Phase 4 — Opportunity Navigation Evolution

## Purpose

Make Opportunities the primary customer-facing commercial workspace while retaining Companies and Contacts as transparent supporting intelligence repositories.

## Changes

- Opportunities moves directly beneath Overview in the revenue workspace.
- Replies is presented as Engagement while its stable route remains unchanged.
- Companies and Contacts move into a separate Intelligence navigation group.
- Overview now prioritises the next ranked opportunity and opportunity metrics.
- Campaigns summarise opportunity counts and pending commercial decisions.
- Campaign detail uses the journey: Business → Campaign → Intelligence → Opportunities → Engagement → Replies → Pipeline.
- Campaign detail links directly to campaign-filtered Opportunity Review.
- Companies and Contacts remain accessible for evidence, audit and correction.

## Frozen boundaries

No database objects, repositories, authentication, workers, scheduler behaviour, evidence storage or opportunity scoring were replaced. This phase changes product language and information architecture only.
