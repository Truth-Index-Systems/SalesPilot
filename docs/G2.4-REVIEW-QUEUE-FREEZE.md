# Genesis G2.4 — Review Queue & Company Discovery Freeze

G2.4 completes the Company Discovery milestone without beginning contact discovery.

## Included

- Search by company, industry or country.
- Campaign, confidence and review-status filters.
- Workspace review totals.
- Individual approval, rejection and return-to-review actions.
- Optional internal review notes.
- Controlled bulk approval and rejection, limited to 100 companies per request.
- Idempotent transactional review RPCs.
- Immutable company review history.
- Customer-visible campaign timeline entries for review decisions.
- Tenant and role validation inside the database function.
- Responsive and keyboard-accessible review queue.

## Migration

Review and manually run `supabase/migrations/0008_genesis_g24_review_queue_freeze.sql` after migrations 0004–0007.

## Scope boundary

No contacts, personal data enrichment, messages, outreach, CRM synchronisation or G3 work is included.
