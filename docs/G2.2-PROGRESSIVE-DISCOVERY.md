# Genesis G2.2 — Progressive Discovery & Live Activity

This release keeps the frozen G1.2 interface and extends G2 with customer-safe live activity and progressive persistence.

## Behaviour

- A campaign launches once and discovery continues automatically.
- The campaign page polls a tenant-scoped status endpoint while discovery is active.
- Activity messages reflect completed backend milestones, not a simulated timer.
- Verified companies are persisted one at a time and become visible in Companies before the overall run finishes.
- Completion is separate from persistence, so a session is marked complete only after at least one company has been saved.
- Contact discovery and outreach remain out of scope.

## Migration

Review and run `supabase/migrations/0006_genesis_g22_progressive_discovery.sql` after migrations 0004 and 0005.
