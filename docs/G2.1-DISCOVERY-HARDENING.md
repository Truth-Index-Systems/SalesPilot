# Genesis G2.1 — Company Discovery Hardening

This patch continues G2 without beginning contact discovery or changing the frozen G1.2 design.

## Delivered

- Worker leases and stale-run recovery.
- Three-attempt retry policy with short exponential backoff.
- Customer-safe manual retry for failed discovery sessions.
- Constant-time CRON secret comparison.
- Both GET and POST worker invocation support.
- Deterministic company-domain normalisation and deduplication.
- Removal of the customer's own company from recommendations.
- Minimum-confidence filtering.
- At least one official-company-site evidence source required per recommendation.
- Consistent confidence-to-match-label presentation.
- Immutable company version increments on a repeated discovery result.
- Evidence replacement rather than duplicate evidence accumulation.
- Idempotent customer timeline completion entry.

## Production test

1. Queue a campaign discovery session.
2. Confirm the worker claims it and sets a lease.
3. Confirm progress reaches ANALYSING, VALIDATING and SAVING.
4. Confirm the customer's own domain is absent.
5. Confirm duplicate domains are absent.
6. Confirm every company has official-site evidence.
7. Force one worker failure and confirm a retry is scheduled.
8. Force a stale RUNNING lease and confirm the next worker invocation recovers it.
9. Exhaust automatic attempts and confirm the campaign displays a safe retry control.
10. Retry as an OWNER or ADMIN and confirm discovery returns to QUEUED.
