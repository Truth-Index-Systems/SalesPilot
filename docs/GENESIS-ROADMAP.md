# Genesis roadmap

## G0 — Foundation (this package)
Campaign-first shell, design system, AI contracts, domain types, events and ports.

## G1 — Tenant foundation
Organisations, memberships, tenant ownership, tenant-scoped RLS and invitations.

## G2 — Live Business Discovery
Website capture, source extraction, evidence store, AI Business DNA proposal and customer approval.

## G3 — Campaign planner
ICP proposals, campaign proposals, campaign versions and launch orchestration.

## G4 — Engine migration
Move proven discovery, evidence, qualification, contact, messaging and delivery services behind campaign workers.

## G5 — Replies and opportunities
Mailbox connection, reply intelligence, follow-ups, opportunity creation and exception-only Focus Mode.

## G6 — Learning
Recommendation lifecycle, outcome memory, Truth Index calibration and strategy proposals.

## G7 — Commercial readiness
Billing, entitlements, usage, integrations, compliance, observability, recovery and controlled beta.
