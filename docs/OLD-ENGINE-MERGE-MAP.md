# Old Engine Merge Map

The previous A–N codebase is a reference library, not the new application shell.

## Carry forward substantially

- Repository and service separation.
- Domain events and transactional outbox.
- Idempotency protections.
- Durable workflow/queue concepts.
- Evidence provenance and freshness.
- Deterministic qualification inputs.
- Contact verification abstraction.
- Immutable approved-draft checksums.
- Recipient-local sending windows and timezone fallback rules.
- Reply classification and transactional side effects.
- Durable follow-up sequences.
- Sales memory extraction concepts.
- Opportunity progression and Focus Mode.

## Reframe behind campaign orchestration

- Company discovery becomes a campaign worker.
- Operational units become hidden business context.
- Qualification becomes the Truth Index decision input.
- Company DNA becomes Business/Company/Campaign DNA.
- Evidence appears contextually rather than as a primary product area.
- Personalisation becomes a message proposal service.
- Workflow Monitor becomes internal system health.
- Delivery Control becomes policy and integration health.
- Memory becomes recommendation context rather than a daily workspace.

## Do not carry forward

- Old navigation and workbench-first customer flow.
- Active-user-wide RLS.
- Global provider credentials.
- Silent demo fallbacks.
- Module-specific manual actions as the normal customer journey.
- Raw AI prose entering the UI.
