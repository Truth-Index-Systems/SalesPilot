# Genesis G3.5 Phase 2 — Opportunity Intelligence

Phase 2 replaces creation-order ranking with a transparent composite commercial score.

## Score

- Company fit: 25%
- Operational fit: 20%
- Buying authority: 20%
- Contactability: 15%
- Evidence quality: 10%
- Commercial value: 5%
- Urgency: 5%

All inputs come from persisted G2/G3 intelligence. The scorer makes no AI or web calls.
Low-scoring opportunities remain visible as `LOW_PRIORITY`; missing contact or evidence is
shown explicitly rather than silently removing the opportunity.
