# Genesis G2.3 — Evidence Validation and Explainable Fit

G2.3 strengthens Company Discovery without beginning contact discovery.

## Production behaviour

1. SalesPilot generates candidates and independent fit dimensions.
2. Every candidate official website is resolved and fetched server-side.
3. Private, local and non-official destinations are rejected before fetch.
4. Evidence URLs must remain on the company's official domain.
5. Unreachable evidence is discarded.
6. Quoted excerpts are checked against retrieved page content when present.
7. SalesPilot calculates evidence quality and recomputes final confidence.
8. Weakly supported candidates are held back rather than shown to customers.
9. Only `VERIFIED` recommendations are persisted and presented.

## Fit dimensions

- Industry fit
- Audience fit
- Operational fit
- Geography fit
- Commercial fit
- Evidence quality

The final confidence score is derived by SalesPilot and is not accepted directly from the model.
