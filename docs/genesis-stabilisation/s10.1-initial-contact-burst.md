# S10.1 — Intelligent initial contact burst

Each campaign may receive one budget-aware burst of fresh contact-discovery jobs. The default balanced policy dispatches up to three jobs concurrently. The scheduler then returns permanently to one contact job per run.

## Invariants

- The burst is persisted once on the campaign.
- Retries are never included.
- A burst requires no running contact job and no due retry for the campaign.
- Workspace request, campaign request, and estimated-cost capacity reduce the burst automatically.
- A constrained first opportunity is recorded as a one-job budget fallback and is not repeated later.
- Database `SKIP LOCKED` claiming prevents duplicate jobs under concurrent execution.
