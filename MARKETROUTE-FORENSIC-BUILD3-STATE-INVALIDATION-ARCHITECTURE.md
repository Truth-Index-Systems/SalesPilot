# MarketRoute — Genesis T8 Forensic Build 3
## State + Authority Invalidation Architecture

**Build:** MR-T8-FB3-1.0.0  
**State lineage version:** MR-T8-FB3-STATE-1.0.0  
**Migration:** `0153_marketroute_forensic_build3_state_invalidation_architecture.sql`  
**Status:** Engineering build complete; not a constitutional freeze.

## 1. Purpose

Build 3 repairs the lifecycle around the Build-2 Commercial Reality producer. The objective is not to change the commercial mathematics. It is to make authority **current, traceable, invalidatable, and state-safe**.

The forensic audit found that an R6 `READY` opportunity could be reset to `BUILDING` by the R4 foundation synchroniser on the next scheduler pass. It also found that R4/R5/R6 decisions had no robust mechanism to become stale when their governing Truth, seller context, route source, contact source, or research basis changed.

Build 3 establishes explicit authority lineage and deterministic invalidation.

## 2. Constitutional invariants introduced

1. **Workflow state is not authority validity.** A row being `READY` does not make its route/contact authority current.
2. **Exact production identity is separate from material authority identity.** Time-aware Truth may generate a new production trace without necessarily changing the commercial decision that downstream systems consume.
3. **Material R4 change invalidates R6 before replacement authority is computed.** Failure to recompute cannot leave stale `READY` authority visible.
4. **R6 source change invalidates R6 independently of R4.** Routes, route evidence, contacts, or contact evidence changing can make a previously valid contact decision stale.
5. **R7 follows the exact R4 research basis.** New Truth/seller/constraint input retires research directives even if the material commercial disposition remains unchanged.
6. **Foundation synchronisation may materialise opportunity identity, but may not demote an opportunity backed by current CIE authority.**
7. **Terminal human/workflow states are protected:** `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, and `ENGAGED` are not automatically overwritten by revalidation.
8. **Authority ledgers are RPC-write-only for the application service role.** Direct DML cannot bypass fingerprint and lineage checks.

## 3. Two-fingerprint architecture

### 3.1 Exact R4 input fingerprint

The existing Build-2 input fingerprint remains the immutable production/research identity. It includes the exact producer version, seller context, constraint set, Truth entity and snapshot identity, Truth calculation time, target facts, active constraints, and deferred constraints.

A different Truth snapshot or reference time therefore creates a different exact trace.

This fingerprint answers:

> “Was this decision produced from exactly the same governed inputs?”

### 3.2 Material R4 authority fingerprint

Build 3 adds `authority_fingerprint` generated by `lib/genesis-t8/cie/authority-lineage.ts`.

It includes only inputs that can materially alter downstream commercial authority:

- seller-context fingerprint;
- seller-constraint fingerprint;
- target Truth entity identity;
- canonical target company identity/facts;
- Commercial Reality identity/state;
- R4 disposition;
- categorical propagation viability;
- eliminating constraints;
- unresolved boundary constraints;
- categorical boundary and limiting states;
- critical stability dimensions.

It deliberately excludes snapshot ID, reference time, and continuous evidence-strength drift.

This fingerprint answers:

> “Has the commercial authority that R5/R6 are permitted to rely on materially changed?”

This distinction prevents time-aware freshness from causing meaningless downstream churn while still preserving an exact audit trail.

## 4. R4 revalidation

The new RPC `get_cie_r4_commercial_reality_revalidation_candidates` selects opportunities when any of these conditions apply:

- R4 authority is missing;
- authority was produced by the Build-2 producer and must be upgraded;
- the material fingerprint is missing;
- seller-context fingerprint changed;
- seller-constraint fingerprint changed;
- a newer TFR1 Truth snapshot exists;
- periodic temporal revalidation is due.

The worker recalculates current TFR1 Truth at one reference time, produces a fresh CE-R2 → R3 → R4 trace, and persists both exact and material fingerprints.

### Stable revalidation

If the exact trace changed but material authority did not:

- the new production is retained;
- R4 `applied_at` is preserved;
- current workflow state is preserved;
- R6 is not invalidated;
- R7 directives tied to the previous exact research basis are retired and may be rebuilt.

### Material revalidation

If material authority changed:

- R6 ACTIVE authority is marked `STALE`;
- R6 `applied_at` is cleared;
- non-terminal opportunity state fails closed to `BUILDING`;
- `primary_contact_id` and legacy `opportunity_score` are cleared;
- R4 is marked unapplied so the new disposition must be applied;
- an append-only R4 invalidation event is written.

## 5. READY → BUILDING regression repair

Build 3 replaces the R4 foundation synchroniser.

Previously, a valid R6 `READY` opportunity could be treated as an R4 foundation mismatch and reset to `BUILDING` during the next scheduler pass.

The new synchroniser distinguishes:

- an opportunity with current CIE production lineage — preserve its downstream state;
- an opportunity with no CIE authority — initialise/fail closed to `BUILDING` awaiting R4.

It may remove a legacy numeric `opportunity_score`, but it no longer overwrites a valid CIE lifecycle state merely because that state is later than R4.

## 6. R6 parent and source invalidation

`cie_r6_contact_decisions` now records:

- `parent_r4_authority_fingerprint`;
- `source_fingerprint`;
- `authority_status` = `ACTIVE | STALE`;
- invalidation time/reason.

Before R6 reads contexts, `invalidate_stale_cie_r6_authority` runs under the active scheduler lease.

An ACTIVE R6 decision becomes STALE if:

- its parent R4 material fingerprint no longer matches;
- R4 is no longer current Build-3 authority;
- a commercial route changed;
- new commercial-route evidence appeared;
- a contact changed;
- new contact evidence appeared.

Invalidation happens **before recomputation**. A failed or unresolved recomputation therefore cannot leave stale READY state in place.

R6 persistence is rejected unless the caller supplies the currently authoritative parent R4 fingerprint and a valid deterministic source fingerprint.

R6 application requires both `authority_status='ACTIVE'` and exact parent-fingerprint equality with current R4.

## 7. R7 exact research lineage

R7 research directives now store `r4_input_fingerprint`.

Research follows the exact current evidence/reasoning basis rather than only the material R4 disposition. A fresh exact R4 trace retires active directives from the previous basis. The R7 writer rejects a stale parent fingerprint, and the retirement RPC removes directives whose R4 input lineage is no longer current.

This prevents research generated for yesterday's evidence state from silently surviving beneath today's authority.

## 8. Append-only invalidation ledger

Build 3 creates `cie_authority_invalidation_events` for deterministic lineage events across:

- R4;
- R6;
- R7.

Events record the opportunity, organisation, campaign, previous and next fingerprints, reason, scheduler run, metadata, and creation time.

This is intended as the forensic record of **why authority stopped being current**, independent of the opportunity's visible workflow state.

## 9. Database authority hardening

Build 3 revokes application service-role direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE access to the CIE authority ledgers. Security-definer RPCs retain controlled write authority.

Old un-fingerprinted R4/R6/R7 writer signatures are removed or revoked from the live service-role surface. PostgREST schema reload is requested at the end of migration 0153.

The Build-2 production table's producer-version CHECK is intentionally evolved to admit both historical `MR-T8-FB2-1.0.0` and current `MR-T8-FB3-1.0.0` rows. Historical provenance is preserved rather than rewritten.

## 10. Migration behaviour for existing Build-2 authority

Migration 0153 does **not** immediately demote existing READY opportunities.

Existing Build-2 R4 decisions are marked due for Build-3 revalidation. Because historical Build-2 rows do not possess a material R4 fingerprint, the first successful Build-3 revalidation is conservative: downstream R6 authority is re-established under the new lineage rather than assumed equivalent.

This avoids both extremes:

- destructive migration-time state resets;
- falsely declaring historical, unfingerprinted authority equivalent to Build-3 authority.

## 11. Scheduler order

The live sequence remains:

1. R4 foundation identity synchronisation;
2. Build-3 Commercial Reality production/revalidation;
3. R4 application;
4. R6 stale-authority invalidation + recomputation/application;
5. R7 counterfactual research loop.

The order guarantees that downstream authority sees current parent state.

## 13. Deliberately deferred issues

Build 3 does **not** legitimise or freeze the existing route/contact inputs.

The forensic audit already established that current R5 still receives `commercial_routes.is_viable`, which originates from legacy G4 weighted route mathematics. R6 also still consumes legacy binary contact-evidence qualification. Build 3 only guarantees that these decisions cannot remain silently current after their sources or parent authority change.

Those issues remain explicitly open:

- **Build 4:** eradicate legacy route authority and remove the hidden G4 weighted `is_viable` decision path;
- **Build 5:** wire the true canonical relationship graph into commercial path reasoning;
- **Build 6:** replace binary legacy contact verification with Truth-qualified contact authority.
