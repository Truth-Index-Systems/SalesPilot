# Genesis T8 CE-R2 R5 — Opportunity Mathematics

R5 orders commercial realities that have already been evaluated by R3/R4. It does not discover companies, interpret semantics, select contacts, invent routes, or calculate truth.

## Ordering law

R5 explicitly rejects a weighted lead score. Ordering is deterministic and hierarchical:

1. **Opportunity realisation state** — `ACTIONABLE` outranks `ACTIONABLE_WITHOUT_NAMED_CONTACT`, which outranks unresolved/stranded/non-viable states. Commercial impossibility cannot be rescued by reachability.
2. **Pareto frontier** — inside the same realisation state, an opportunity outranks another if it is no worse on every deterministic R4 state axis and strictly better on at least one.
3. **Maximin opportunity robustness** — incomparable members of the same Pareto frontier are ordered by the strength of their weakest decision-relevant axis.
4. **Commercial strength, then decision assurance** — used only when maximin robustness ties.
5. **Canonical opportunity ID** — used only when no mathematical preference remains, solely for reproducibility.

## State separation

Commercial strength is conservative:

`min(Commercial Coherence, Commercial Stability, Constraint Headroom)`

Decision assurance is epistemic:

`min(Knowledge Sufficiency, Reasoning Confidence)`

Opportunity robustness is the weaker of those two states. No mean can hide a severe weakness behind several strong dimensions.

## Route/contact boundary

R5 consumes only R4's categorical realisation state. It does not assign numeric values to contacts or routes. A direct named-person route therefore affects the categorical opportunity state, not a hidden route weight.

## MarketRoute top three

The application may display the first three ordered opportunities. The number `3`, free-tier policy, pricing, and UI presentation are not part of UDOSIB mathematics.
