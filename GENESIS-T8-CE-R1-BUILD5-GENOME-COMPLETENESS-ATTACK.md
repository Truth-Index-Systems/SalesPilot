# Genesis T8 — Commercial Engine
## CE Release 1 / Build 5 — Commercial Genome Completeness Attack

### Status
Pre-freeze ontology hardening. No production runtime activation.

### Purpose
Build 5 attacks the Build 4 Commercial Genome instead of extending it speculatively. The release gate is representability: can Genesis T8 express the objective, truth-qualifiable facts needed to reason about materially different organisations and commercial constraints without smuggling fit, priority, recommendation or contact reasoning into the ontology?

### Adversarial coverage
The deterministic fixture suite spans enterprise SaaS, manufacturing, logistics, retail/ecommerce, regulated healthcare, public-sector buying, professional services, field service/construction, regulated fintech, venture startups, wholesale/distribution and multinational enterprises.

It also tests cross-industry reasoning prerequisites for geography, scale, technical compatibility, procurement, transaction economics, organisational function ownership, operating complexity, regulation, delivery mechanics, momentum and ecosystem context.

### Gaps proven and closed
The Build 4 catalogue was strong but underrepresented several objective constraint surfaces. Build 5 adds canonical predicates for legal/ownership structure, delivery mechanics, physical operating scale, customer economics, budget and vendor-onboarding behaviour, organisational function ownership, deployment/integration technology, fiscal context, additional regulatory constraints, dynamic events and ecosystem intermediaries.

The organisation family intentionally stops at business-function ownership. It does not identify or rank people; that remains the future Contact Engine responsibility.

### Responsibility boundary
- AI may discover candidate values and evidence for canonical predicates.
- TI-2.1.8 alone qualifies truth, confidence and coverage.
- The Commercial Genome defines what facts can exist.
- Build 5 tests representability only; it calculates no commercial fit.
- Commercial mathematics remains a later release.

### Freeze rule
Passing Build 5 is necessary but not sufficient to freeze Commercial Genome v1. Build 6 still defines the AI research contract and Build 7 performs the final freeze audit.
