# Genesis T8 CE-R2 R4 — Commercial Coherence & Opportunity Realisation

## Purpose
R4 composes R3's propagated constraint state into deterministic commercial coherence, constraint pressure, commercial stability, knowledge sufficiency, reasoning confidence, dimension-level coherence, and opportunity realisation.

## Separation of concerns
Commercial viability is foundational. Contact and route availability may determine whether a surviving commercial reality can be realised, but they can never rescue a commercially eliminated target or inflate underlying Commercial Coherence.

AI owns semantic grouping and the semantic classification of contact/route states. R4 accepts no AI-supplied numeric weights, fit scores, probabilities, or rankings.

## Reinforcement
Constraints that AI identifies as representing the same semantic proposition family share a `reinforcementGroupKey`. Within a group R4 uses the maximum effective force, never the sum. Distinct groups reinforce with bounded diminishing returns:

`R = 1 - Π(1 - g_j)`

This permits genuinely independent commercial signals to reinforce each other while preventing synonyms, duplicate paths, or repeated representations from manufacturing extra force.

## Constraint pressure
Independent limiting and contradiction channels are composed as:

`P = 1 - (1 - L)(1 - X)`

where `L` is bounded limiting pressure and `X` is TI-owned contradiction uncertainty after R3 relevance propagation.

## Commercial coherence
For a reality that survives R3:

`C = S(1 - P)`

where `S` is bounded reinforced support and `P` is constraint pressure. Eliminated or unresolved commercial realities have no final R4 coherence output.

## Commercial stability
Commercial Stability is the distance to the nearest active Boundary constraint after survival, elimination and relevant contradiction forces are considered. A surviving reality with no active boundary constraints has stability 1 by convention.

## Knowledge
Knowledge Sufficiency remains orthogonal to fit. R4 uses the weakest active semantic group's represented knowledge rather than allowing many well-known trivial facts to average away one critical unknown. Reasoning Confidence is then derived from knowledge sufficiency and unresolved contradiction, never from commercial attractiveness.

## Contact and route interfaces
R4 does not discover a contact or route. It consumes categorical interfaces reserved for future Contact and Route engines.

Contact states: `APPROPRIATE`, `PLAUSIBLE`, `UNKNOWN`, `INAPPROPRIATE`.

Route states: `DIRECT`, `INDIRECT`, `WEAK`, `UNKNOWN`, `BLOCKED`.

Route target modes: `PERSON`, `ORGANISATION`, `INTERMEDIARY`.

A company can therefore be commercially coherent but `STRANDED` if no viable route exists. Conversely, an organisational or intermediary route may be actionable without a named contact. Reachability never repairs commercial impossibility.
