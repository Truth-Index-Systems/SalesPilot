# Genesis G4 Testing Polish

- Company Discovery retries after 30 seconds, then 2 minutes; a third failure moves to Needs Attention.
- Campaign-stage language is consistent: Company Discovery, Route Research, Opportunity Review, Engagement.
- Business-understanding confidence now shows the evidence behind the label.
- “Worth confirming” is collapsed by default with an item count.
- Campaign strategy cards have improved section spacing.
- Customer and Founder Dashboard timelines use a shared bounded component with scrolling and Today / This week / This month / All time filters.
- Timeline entries are displayed chronologically within the chosen period.
