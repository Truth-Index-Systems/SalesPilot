# Genesis G4 — Company Discovery Search Order

Company Discovery now separates market discovery from qualification.

1. Build a structured search plan from the campaign and Business DNA.
2. Translate the offer into operational conditions and company archetypes.
3. Search broadly across diverse archetypes and terminology.
4. Build a candidate pool of up to 20 companies.
5. Prioritise operations, locations, careers, reports, procurement, case studies and official news before homepages.
6. Independently verify official evidence and commercial fit.
7. Retain supported matches or continue the existing bounded expansion loop.

The evidence gate is unchanged. Search breadth is improved upstream rather than weakening verification downstream.
