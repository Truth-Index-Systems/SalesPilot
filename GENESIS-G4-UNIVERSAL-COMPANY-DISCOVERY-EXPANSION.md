# Genesis G4 — Universal Company Discovery Expansion

The minimum-three supported-company objective now applies to every active Company Discovery cycle, not only onboarding or the first campaign run.

- Each clean pass is evaluated against the same target of three supported companies.
- MarketRoute automatically broadens the search for up to four passes.
- Evidence thresholds, deduplication and AI governance remain unchanged.
- Active historical campaigns that stopped below target are resumed when they still have unused expansion passes.
- Paused, cancelled, archived and already-exhausted campaigns are not restarted.
