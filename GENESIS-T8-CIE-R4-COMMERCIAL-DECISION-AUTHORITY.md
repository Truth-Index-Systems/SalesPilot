# Genesis T8 — CIE-R4 Commercial Decision Authority

CIE-R4 performs the first live authority cutover. Commercial Reality + CE2 deterministic state now own commercial disposition. The legacy opportunity scorer is explicitly fail-closed and is no longer invoked by the scheduler.

R4 intentionally cannot unlock engagement. Route and contact authority remain scheduled for CIE-R5/R6. An ESTABLISHED commercial reality is therefore persisted as `COMMERCIAL_CANDIDATE` but remains `BUILDING` in the legacy workflow until those later constitutional owners migrate.

This release does not infer CIE truth/semantic inputs from old opportunity scores. Missing authoritative CIE decision input means no decision is applied. There is no legacy fallback.
