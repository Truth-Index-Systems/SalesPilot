# MarketRoute Product Prestige Pass

## Intent
Presentation-only release. No changes to Genesis T8, Truth Index, UDOSIB, campaign orchestration, ranking authority, AI governance, persistence semantics, or background workers.

## Product goals
- Make MarketRoute feel powerful without becoming visually noisy.
- Increase perceived trust, reliability and engineering maturity.
- Create stronger hierarchy between decisions, evidence and background activity.
- Use restrained motion, tighter typography and executive-grade spacing.
- Preserve every existing workflow and route.

## Changes
- Reworked global visual tokens: canvas, surfaces, typography contrast, borders and shadow system.
- Premium dark application navigation with quieter hierarchy and better active states.
- Frosted top application chrome and improved workspace status presentation.
- Stronger page-title hierarchy and more consistent information density.
- Executive-grade primary hero surface for next-action and progress states.
- Refined card, metric, table, list, evidence and form presentation.
- More deliberate button hierarchy and hover/focus states.
- Upgraded company/result cards and filter controls.
- High-end public landing treatment: hero, product preview, trust blocks, steps, security and CTA surfaces.
- Preserved reduced-motion accessibility and mobile behaviour.

## Constitutional impact
None. CE-R1 and CE-R2 are untouched.
