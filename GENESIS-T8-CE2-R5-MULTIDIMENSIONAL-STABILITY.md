# Genesis T8 — CE2-R5 Multidimensional Commercial Stability

## Research question
What mathematical relationship should CE2 communicate when it says a surviving Commercial Reality is "stable"?

The required relationship is not average quality. It is resistance to commercial failure across multiple non-substitutable axes: a severe weakness must not be hidden by strong unrelated dimensions; equal weakest margins should still be distinguishable when one reality has additional fragility behind the weakest point; and future dynamic survivability should only be modelled once admissible perturbations/actions are explicitly defined.

## Candidate 1 — robust optimisation / maximin
Strong fit for the constitutional safety floor. Robust optimisation evaluates decisions against adverse/worst-case realizations. CE2 already embodies the same principle through nearest-boundary stability and maximin opportunity robustness.

Synthetic result: safe and non-compensatory, but lossy. `[0.20,0.90,0.90,0.90]` and `[0.20,0.25,0.90,0.90]` both reduce to `0.20`.

## Candidate 2 — viability theory
Strong conceptual fit for future dynamic stability. Viability theory asks whether a system can remain inside an admissible constraint set as it evolves. This maps naturally to Commercial Reality remaining commercially viable over time.

Synthetic result: not suitable as the primary R5 implementation because a viability kernel requires an explicit dynamics/action/disturbance model. CE2 does not yet possess a constitutional commercial perturbation model, so implementing one now would require invented assumptions.

## Candidate 3 — lexicographic maximin
Best fit for multidimensional static stability. Compare the weakest margin first; if tied, compare the second weakest; continue until a difference exists. This retains maximin's non-compensation while preserving fragility information that a scalar minimum loses.

Synthetic result: correctly distinguishes `[0.20,0.90,0.90,0.90]` from `[0.20,0.25,0.90,0.90]` without introducing weights or averages.

## CE2-R5 decision
Use a hybrid:

1. **Robust floor** = minimum dimension margin. This remains the canonical scalar safety floor.
2. **Lexicographic stability profile** = all dimension margins sorted ascending. This is the deterministic non-compensatory comparison object.
3. **Viability theory deferred** = future extension after Genesis has an explicit admissible perturbation/action transition model.

## Stability dimensions
R5 does not invent Operational/Financial/Market/etc. categories. It reuses the six already-authorised CE-R1 coherence dimensions currently mapped to commercial constraints: Semantic, Structural, Operational, Commercial, Technological and Strategic. Truth remains Truth Index-owned; Temporal remains R3-owned; Relational is deferred to graph/route mathematics rather than invented as a numeric stability weight.

## Constraint-to-margin rules
Boundary margin uses frozen CE-R2 boundary survival minus elimination minus contradiction. Limiting margin is remaining headroom after limiting pressure and contradiction. Contradictory constraints consume contradiction headroom. Supporting and Unknown constraints do not create stability force: support cannot manufacture resilience, and unknownness remains epistemic under R2/R4.

## Constitutional result
R5 adds multidimensional stability without modifying frozen UDOSIB v1, changing opportunity rankings, inventing weights, or moving truth/time semantics into the stability layer.
